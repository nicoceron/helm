# Art Bible

## Prototype direction

The prototype is asset-free and code-drawn:

- near-black orbital gradient;
- mint civic signal lines;
- clipped-corner glass policy card;
- geometric lion mark;
- grid, orbit, stacked-city, and star geometry;
- advisor monograms rendered as holographic signal IDs;
- procedural ambient loop and synthetic policy cues.

This keeps the visual language original and makes the repository immediately runnable without license risk.

## Color and shape language

| System | Shape | Color role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cohesion | circle `●` | warm amber |
| Growth | grid `▦` | electric cyan |
| Security | triangle `▲` | alert coral |
| Autonomy | orbit `◉` | resilient green |

High-contrast mode converts danger and card outlines to white. Shapes and text labels remain present, so no state depends on hue alone.

## Final-art handoff

Commission original character portraits, backgrounds, ending illustrations, icons, music, and effects. Retain the civic-hologram composition and avoid beige medieval cards or any recognizable franchise framing. Major characters need neutral, worried, angry, pleased, silhouette, and small-head variants. Arden requires three age states.
