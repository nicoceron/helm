# Writing and Sensitivity Guide

## Voice

Copy is crisp, tense, dryly funny, and bureaucratic. A speaker identifies the pressure; the card never gives a lecture. Prompts are at most 120 characters in data and choices at most 20 characters.

Good jokes expose systems:

- paperwork behaving like weather;
- infrastructure acquiring political opinions;
- officials using precise language to describe absurd risk;
- citizens puncturing grand strategy with material reality.

## Trade-offs

Neither direction is “good.” Each standard option must change at least two systems and must contain both benefit and cost. Crisis choices may be uniformly painful, but still need a meaningful distinction.

Later consequences should name the player’s earlier decision through the situation, not through a morality tooltip. `autonomy_001`, for example, only becomes eligible after signing the earlier ice treaty and is marked as a returning decision in the UI.

## IP guardrails

- Do not use the Reigns name in player-facing content.
- Do not imitate its card frame, medieval palette, characters, copy, jokes, icons, music, or layout.
- Do not quote real political speeches.
- Do not map real ethnic groups to alien species.
- Historical parallels belong in metadata and optional source notes, never as a renamed one-to-one retelling.

## Historical care

Show capacity and cost together. Housing can raise living standards while displacing communities. Security can deter threats while weakening liberties. Foreign investment can build skills while creating capture. Common working language can improve mobility while stressing identity. Succession is a system test, not a verdict on one person.
