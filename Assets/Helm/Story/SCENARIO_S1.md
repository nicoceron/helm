# Scenario S1 - Big Brother Is Watching

## Player promise

You are not inheriting a fantasy kingdom. You are sitting the HELM ruler-capability examination. In sixteen policy decisions, two playable crises, and roughly eight to twelve minutes, you must turn Lionrise from Earth's poorest ration district into a resilient first-world city.

HELM grades outcomes and methods. A beautiful skyline is insufficient if the system depends on fear, cannot hear street-level reality, or collapses when the ruler is wrong.

Before the first policy choice, ORISON-9 leads a seven-beat character briefing that establishes the examiner, planetary city, orbital class divide, conditions in Lionrise, the twenty-year simulation, the four gauges, and the swipe preview. Pip then carries the human cost of development from the pressure rooms to the schools and council chamber. The first governing swipe is Central's forced withdrawal, not unexplained exposition.

## Setting

The year is 3024. Earth is a connected planetary city of roughly thirty billion people. Central Governance and its AI systems monitor commerce, movement, education, identity, and resource distribution. Elites inhabit privileged orbital settlements while lower districts endure rationing, blackouts, ecological decline, and manufactured precarity.

Lionrise occupies a strategically valuable equatorial sector around an abandoned space-elevator route. Central withdraws its water subsidy and patrols, forcing the sector into self-rule. The candidate has twenty simulated years to make it secure, prosperous, habitable, and governable.

12. Garden-city investment
13. Meritocracy and upward mobility
14. Surveillance, safety, and oversight

The four-card introduction ends by explaining the top gauges and swipe preview. The policy sequence is interrupted by a roughly thirty-two-second signal-guitar performance at the equatorial elevator and a twelve-second allocation-pulse stabilization game inside the Civic Eye. The concert requires at least 20 of its 28 signal beats, and successful beats restore only one lost health level. Eight text interludes carry the story between eras without inventing meaningless binary choices or changing the background on every card.

## Capability rubric

- Power measures grid strength and administrative execution.
- Oxygen measures water security and biosphere resilience.
- People measures welfare, confidence, and corrective feedback.
- Hull measures infrastructure and productive capacity.

The final verdict uses these four visible gauges plus the candidate's last crisis response. A balanced open system earns distinction. Strong command and infrastructure can produce a conditional Glass Citadel pass, while strong productive or living systems lead to narrower passing outcomes. There is no invisible score deciding the result.

## Scenario S1 source alignment

The scenario preserves the supplied brief's core conditions: rule by one, hierarchical order, scarcity distribution, pervasive nano-infrastructure, AI monitoring, elite orbital settlements, biosphere decline, monumental technospheric expansion, and the risk of cascading failure hidden from a convinced philosopher ruler.
